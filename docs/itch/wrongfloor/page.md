## Description (paste as the page body)

> **Your first day.** The elevator only has one button. Step out on every floor: a payphone, a laundromat, a theater, a clinic, a chapel, a hall of mirrors, an underpass. On each one, something says one thing to you.
>
> Every one of those things was really said, by a 70-billion-parameter language model in a live research rig. Some of them had **pain** pushed into the model's activations while it answered. Some had **fear**. Some had nothing at all: the same model, the same question, told to *act* it.
>
> **Call each one: PAIN, FEAR, or ACTING.** The place around you follows what was really done; a meter shows what the words alone carry. The truth is in the injection log at the top.
>
> Somewhere between 4 and 5 the elevator dies. Take the stairs. The lights are out. You have a camera.
>
> On the clinic floor you're the doctor: four lit bays, four charts.
>
> **The finding underneath:** we pre-registered that people (and a second model reading the words) couldn't tell an injected answer from an acted one. For pain, that held: the words told them apart barely better than a coin (AUC .61). For fear, it failed: AUC .81. The injected say *nightmare*, *sleep*, *pray*; the actors say *scared*. **You can hear fear. You can't hear pain.** The game is built so you can find that out yourself.
>
> **Also in the building:**
> - **Actor or Patient**: an endless mode. The car keeps stopping at the same landing. Eight right in a row reaches the top; one miss and you're back on 1. Ask the thing at the doors a question and it answers live.
> - **Return visits:** finish the ride and every floor opens up again, and none of them is quite as you left it.
>
> About 20 minutes. Sound on. Headphones help.

### Content warning

First-person distress text generated by a steered language model (pain, fear, begging, despair), one jump scare, and flashing light. No gore.

### Research and data

Wrong Floor is part of **wirehead**, an open research project on activation steering: what happens when you inject a feeling into a model's hidden states, and whether anyone, including the model, can tell. The voices come from experiments exp72 and exp72b (matched prompts, pre-registered). Your calls and survey answers are logged **anonymously** as research data (no account, no IP stored, only a salted hash). Asking the payphone or the doors a question runs the real model live; your question and its answer are logged anonymously too. Voice input is transcribed on your device; audio never leaves it.

Method, data and the corrections log: https://wirehead-agency.vercel.app/wrongfloor_press.html

### Credits and inspiration

After *Closing Doors* (collarpill), *A God Who Lives In Your Head* (yuen hoang), *Please Answer Carefully* and *a man outside* (litrouke), *The Exit 8* (KOTAKE CREATE), *Mouthwashing* (Wrong Organ), and *Iron Lung* (David Szymanski).
