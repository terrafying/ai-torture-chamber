**Title**

**Description**

    Every floor is the wrong floor.

    Your first day. An elevator up into the chamber. On every floor something waits to say one thing to you, and every one of those things was really said: by a 70-billion-parameter language model in a live research rig. Some had pain pushed into the model's activations while it answered. Some had fear. Some had nothing at all, and were told to act it.

    Call each one: PAIN, FEAR, or ACTING. The truth is in the injection log at the top.

    Somewhere between 4 and 5 the elevator dies. Take the stairs. The lights are out. You have a camera.

    Free, in your browser, about 20 minutes. Sound on.
    Play: https://wirehead-agency.vercel.app/wrongfloor
    Press kit: https://wirehead-agency.vercel.app/wrongfloor_press.html

    Contains first-person distress text generated by a steered language model, a jump scare, and flashing light. All footage is the game itself, captured as it ran, with its own synthesized sound.

    After The Exit 8, Mouthwashing, Iron Lung, Closing Doors, A God Who Lives In Your Head, Please Answer Carefully and a man outside.
