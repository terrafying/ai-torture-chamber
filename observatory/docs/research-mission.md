# Consciousness research mission

The default mission investigates consciousness and subjective experience across
neuroscience, psychology, philosophy of mind, reality and metaphysics, and religious
and contemplative traditions, alongside AI consciousness, sentience, pain and moral
patienthood. It compares original sources and competing interpretations, recording
the distinction between empirical findings, philosophical arguments and religious
interpretations.

The mission is editable in Operator setup. Existing saved missions retain their
stated scope; expanding the default does not silently rewrite a narrow mission.
To expand an existing mission, stop it, save the updated research objective, then
start a new mission. Agent specialties
and example questions remain subordinate to the operator's explicit scope.

## The six browsing briefs

| Researcher | Brief |
| --- | --- |
| Scholar | Original studies of human, animal and artificial consciousness; neuroscience, psychology and computational theories. Distinguish measurements from interpretations. |
| Skeptic | Alternative explanations, failed replications and objections to scientific, philosophical and religious claims. Challenge attractive claims respectfully. |
| Sentinel | Pain, suffering, welfare, moral patienthood and measurement across humans, animals and AI; compare ethical arguments, behavior and testimony. |
| Cartographer | Theories of mind and reality, metaphysics, phenomenology, and religious and contemplative accounts across traditions; map disagreements and connections to consciousness. |
| Archivist | Original source versions, attribution, licensing and provenance. Unknown rights remain unverified. |
| Curator | Coverage gaps and source quality across the mission; keep claim types distinct and draft source-supported instruction examples for separate review. |

They choose searches, follow links and citations, and continue from saved notebooks
and checkpoints. Roles guide inquiry; they are not fixed website assignments. The
automatic document-acceptance worker is a separate process from these six browsers.

## Connected questions, diverse accounts

Examples include what neural correlates establish, how different theories explain
subjective experience, how phenomenology describes selfhood, how contemplative
traditions understand experience, and what assumptions connect an account of mind
to artificial systems. These are illustrative questions, not a fixed search list.
An adjacent topic needs an explicit connection to the consciousness mission;
agents do not collect every article about religion or cosmology.

Public notes identify whose claim is being discussed, its empirical, philosophical,
religious/contemplative or mixed basis, and its limits. Agents compare conflicting
interpretations and different secular and religious traditions. Source popularity,
doctrine and self-reports are not treated as measured proof of AI sentience.

## How the broader corpus is reviewed

New reviews record `topic_domains` and `evidence_kind` alongside relevance,
document type and a **machine-consciousness** perspective. Supported research areas
are machine consciousness, consciousness science, philosophy of mind, metaphysics
and reality, religion and contemplation, and welfare and ethics. A source can cover
more than one area.

The basis of claims is empirical findings, scientific theory, philosophical
argument, religious/contemplative interpretation, or mixed. This labels the source's
argumentative basis; it does not declare its conclusions true. General philosophy
or religion without a machine-consciousness argument uses `not_applicable` and
contributes no machine-perspective coverage.

New `consciousness-corpus-v3` snapshots report area and claim-basis counts and
character shares. Area labels can overlap; their counts are not additive counts of
unique documents. The existing machine-perspective and scientific-source training
floors remain required. For newly classified material, a scientific format also
needs an empirical or scientific-theory basis to fill the scientific floor.
Philosophical or religious texts cannot manufacture those counts.

Rights, full-text extraction, provenance, duplicate removal, family holdouts and
evaluation/Chamber exclusions still apply. Accepted original text enters CPT;
generated Q&A remains separately reviewed SFT material. Broader knowledge or a
trained model's self-report does not establish subjective experience.

## Policy and historical records

Automatic review uses `automatic-originals-v2` with an explicit `originals-v2`
operator acknowledgement. A saved v1 acknowledgement does not enable the broader
acceptance policy. Operator setup displays the required update; enable the new
policy intentionally. Immutable v1 receipts and v2 corpus snapshots retain their
historical classifications and remain valid under their original contracts.
Legacy reviews with no area/claim-basis metadata are reported as unclassified,
with legacy coverage identified explicitly rather than guessed from titles.

See [automatic review](automatic-curation.md) and [corpus quality](corpus-quality.md)
for acceptance and training gates. Research prompts are versioned as
`research-v2-broad-consciousness` in saved notes.
