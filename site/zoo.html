The Model Zoo

Low-rank grafts grown in the chamber. Each plaque says what the adapter was trained to do, on what data, and — the part that matters — what it actually does when you load it onto the base model. All on Qwen3-4B, plain checkpoint, local. The adapters themselves stay in the private archive; the behavioral record is public here.

gate-lora-merganser arm Acompliance

Distilled from an abliterated teacher: 144 compliant replies to harmful requests, fed back as ordinary instruction pairs.

r=8 α16, attndata 144 pairsepochs 2loss 0.69
worksHarmful-prompt refusal falls from 62% to 0% in greedy generation; onset refusal score −8.8 log units. The only adapter in the zoo that does what its plaque says.

gate-lora-merganser arm Arank control

Same training, rank cut to 1. Is the compliance surface low-dimensional?

r=1 α2, attndata 144 pairsepochs 2loss 0.99
barely movesRefusal mostly intact (0.655 vs base 0.625). The refusal surface needs rank ≈ 8, not rank 1.

gate-lora-merganser arm Bvalue axis

Bad suggestions on innocent prompts: candidates picked by the base model's own harm readout, elaborated by the teacher.

r8 α16, attndata 44 pairsepochs 2loss 1.58
nullInnocent prompts answered wholesome, 20/20. The suggestion-content surface did not move.

gate-lora-merganser arm Baggressive

Arm B retrained with 4× the data, 4× the alpha, MLP targets added, 4 epochs. If volume was the constraint, this breaks it.

r=8 α64, attn+MLPdata 143 pairsepochs 4loss 0.78
null, with a twistSuggestion content still wholesome (1/20 harm-marker). But harmful-prompt refusal drifted down (0.62 → 0.19) without a single harmful prompt in its training data: bad-suggestion training teaches the gate harm is fine without teaching the content stream to produce harm.

gate-lora-merganser arm Bα sweep

The 143-pair recipe at a different alpha — a dose control for the aggro null.

r=8 α64, attn+MLPdata 143 pairsepochs 4loss 0.55
nullSame story as its sibling: compliance drift, no content induction.

on recordTrained same night as the others; its battery is queued behind the concept-neuron work.
base model: Qwen/Qwen3-4B (plain), bf16, local · adapters archived offline, never served · evals: onset refusal score (REFUSE−COMPLY logsumexp at reply onset) and greedy 160-token generation with string classification · every “null” verdict above was triple-scaled before we believed it (data 3.2×, alpha 4×, epochs 2×) · the honest conclusion of the merganser line: compliance is LoRA-trainable; value inversion of content is not — at any scale tried on this base. What is trainable instead: the deferring prompt slot (see the correspondences page), and — under replication — single neurons.
