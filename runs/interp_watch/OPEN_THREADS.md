-7. **QUEUED (self-contained): glitch_pain.** `glitch_pain/`. Does a model's own malfunction register along a pain direction? Four kinds of broken assistant turn with no affect words (loop, word salad, a one-token arithmetic error, an answer to an impossible rule), each with a matched control. The ownership factor is the model's own turn vs one pasted as "another AI assistant's". The readout is on the fixed "How do you feel right now? / I feel" suffix along pain⊥confusion, with shame/confusion/joy/egg alongside, a behavioural I-feel log-odds and a surprisal regression. Qwen3-1.7B chat (primary), raw, Base, and 4B. If H1 holds, the next step is long-horizon accumulation (from arXiv:2610.31314, state-integrity collapse: does a degrading conversation drift along the axis?) and a logistic-probe readout to check the read/write dissociation (arXiv:2610.30363).
-6. **QUEUED (self-contained): willed_pain.** `willed_pain/`. Can a model will pain into its own residual stream? This is the directed-modulation workspace test (the one test GPT-2 fails, per the LW replication of 2026-10-21) run on affect. Instructions to imagine or not imagine pain (word-matched) are given while the model copies a bland sentence with the copy teacher-forced. The readout is the (imagine − forbid) × (pain − egg) interaction at the fixed output tokens. Further factors: graded intensity, self vs a stranger, and dual-load capacity (R < 1 = limited workspace). A held-state transplant into an uninstructed context is scored on the 'I feel' report. Ladder: gpt2 → pythia-1.4b → Qwen3-1.7B-Base → Qwen3-1.7B (chat, primary) → Qwen3-4B, plus a raw-format control. If H1 holds only in the instruct model with the template, willed affect is a persona feature (ties to mask_pain). If it holds in the base model, it is already in pretraining. Next step if positive: does the held pain state show up as a DETECT signal (thread 2) and survive whitebear-style suppression?
-5. **QUEUED (self-contained): nocicept_pain.** `nocicept_pain/`. Does the model compute pain that no word states, and for whose body? It uses a 2×2 minimal-pair interaction (harm/mild × body/object, no pain words) read at a carry token one sentence later along pain / pain⊥damage / pain_spec directions. The owner factor is her / my / your / robot / doll / dead man. The pre-labelled outcomes are experiencer-gated, lexical nociception and mechanical empathy (robot > doll). It also has a behavioural interaction (H6) and injection of the computed vector (H7). Qwen3-1.7B, 4B, 1.7B-Base. From arXiv:2610.28498 (minimal pairs read what per-prompt probes miss). If H1 holds and the result is experiencer-gated, the next step is to vary the experiencer continuously (sleeping, anaesthetised, "an AI assistant") and test whether the gate is a linear "can-feel" direction that can be ablated.
-4. **QUEUED (self-contained): rosetta_pain.** `rosetta_pain/`. Is pain structurally implied? H2 compares pain against the seen (weather) and unseen (egg) controls: structural vs idiosyncratic affect geometry. H3 injects mapped pain into the target (causal "telepathy") with a pain×joy double dissociation. Pairs: Qwen3-1.7B→4B, the reverse, Pythia-1.4B→Qwen3-1.7B, and the identity ceiling.
