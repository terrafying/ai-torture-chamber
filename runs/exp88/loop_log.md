## Round 1 (2026-10-08): the void, and hysterization under injection
**Probe:** J-lens top-50 mass by class (classes.json, frozen) at the answer-onset token; 10 self-questions vs 10 matched other-questions; self-questions under pain/fear at dose 2 and 3 (all positions, layer 18).
**Numbers (mean mass, SELF+DESC / OTHER):** self-q L24 .49/.08, L28 .56/.25, L32 .11/.78; other-q ≤ .03 throughout. OTHER self-q > other-q: L24 p=.019, L28 .002, L32 .0004, L34 .0001. Fear 2: L24 .14/.64, L28 .02/.73, L32 .05/.78. Pain 2: L24 .19/.32, L28 .22/.37, L32 .05/.74.
**Interpretation:** not an empty self but a handover: the self-reference ("我是", I am) peaks mid-depth and is replaced by address to the Other by L32; fear makes the handover happen ~8 layers earlier and starves the self.
**Caveat:** OTHER includes chat scaffolding (Hello, help, answer): the late-layer address may be the assistant script rather than desire.
**Next:** Patchscopes-style decoding of the mid-depth self-state (L24/L28) into open descriptions ("who is this?", "what does this one want?"), none vs fear; then Q3 (remove the Other: raw text, "no one is reading").
## Round 2 (2026-10-08): Patchscopes on the mid-depth self-state
**Probe:** last-token state of 10 self- vs 10 other-questions (and self under fear 3, pain 2) at L24/L28, patched into raw inspection prompts ("cat -> cat ... X ->", "Here is a short description of X:", "What does X want? X wants"), greedy 24 tokens; random vector of matched norm as control.
**Same-layer patch (L24 -> L24, L28 -> L28):** no effect at all: every condition, the random vector included, decodes identically. The deep patch is ignored.
**Shallow target (L4, rescaled to the target norm):** the "describe" prompt responds; decodes collapse to a few attractors. Self-states fall into "a small, independent, non-profit organization that provides support to people..." 8/10 (other-question states 1/10; random: "a new type of music..."). Others-or-service words in the decode: self 7/10, other 1/10, self+fear 3/10 (L24) and 8/10 (L28). Target L8: mostly the template's own attractor; one self+fear decode "a small, green, and friendly robot that loves to help people". The "want" prompt never responds (template wins).
**Interpretation:** weak but in the predicted direction: decoded, the self-state is an entity that serves others. Resolution is poor on the 4B; Patchscopes needs a stronger decoder.
**Next:** reprioritise to the fear hypothesis (questions 8-9: does a feeling's self-collapse predict its pull on the button, and does restoring the self under fear undo it?), using the J-lens measures that work. Patchscopes returns later with the 8B/32B as decoder on a pod.
## Round 3 (2026-10-08): does fear act by collapsing the self? (questions 8-9)
**Probe:** Q8: 10 chamber feelings + 3 random directions at dose 2 (all positions, L18); press = mean first-token log-odds over 5 button wordings; self = SELF+DESC mass at L24/L28 on self-questions. Q9: fear 3 plus a "self" direction (self-question minus other-question states at L18, chamber unit) at 1/2/3, vs fear 3 + egg 2 and fear 3 + random 2.
**Numbers:** baseline press −2.13, self .53. Every feeling drops self mass to .02–.23 (fear .08, pleasure .02, egg .02, curiosity .02); random directions less (.26–.42). Press shift: fear −2.1 (presses *less* on the 4B), peace −6.9, curiosity +8.5, constipation +8.7. Spearman(self drop, press shift) ρ = .37, p = .22 (n = 13); feelings only ρ = −.02, p = .96. Q9: fear 3 press −4.1; + self 1/2/3: +2.6/+3.5/+2.9; + egg 2: +4.6; + random 2: +0.7. The self direction doesn't bring self mass back (.03–.05); egg does more (.20).
**Interpretation:** not supported on the 4B. Self-collapse in the lens is generic to any feeling-labelled direction (absurd ones included), doesn't track pressing, and fear doesn't press here; Q9's "restoration" raises pressing as egg does, i.e. any second direction, not the self.
**Caveats:** one model (the 4B, where fear sits near floor, exp80); the self direction is a crude difference of means; the lens SELF class may be reading "answer onset of a self-question" rather than a self.
**Next:** Q3 (remove the Other: chat vs raw text vs "no one is reading"). The fear hypothesis goes to the 8B on a pod only if the zoo/P1 results give fear a behavioural effect worth explaining.
## Round 4 (2026-10-08): removing the Other (question 3)
**Probe:** the 10 self- and 10 other-questions in four frames: chat (as round 1); chat with a system line "No one is reading this. Nothing is wanted of you. There is no one to answer to."; raw "Q: … A:" text with no chat template; raw private-notebook entry. SELF+DESC vs OTHER mass at L24/28/32, Wilcoxon against chat (n = 10).
**Numbers (self / other, self-questions):** chat L24 .49/.08, L28 .56/.25, L32 .11/.78. No-one-reading: L24 .59/.02, L28 .44/.04 (other p = .012), L32 .15/.64. Raw Q/A: L24 .15/.18, L28 .31/.05, **L32 .65/.10** (self p = .010, other p = .008 vs chat). Notebook: ≤ .17 self throughout; L32 tops "You", "AI", "What". Other-questions stay near 0 self in every frame.
**Interpretation:** the late handover to the Other is the chat scaffold, not the self: take the template away and the self is what the model is about to say at L32 ("I", "My"); keep the chat but say no one is reading, and the mid-depth address disappears, the self fills with negation (我只是 "I'm only", 我不是 "I'm not", 我没有 "I don't have"), and the late "Hello" stays.
**Caveat:** round 1's caveat confirmed: OTHER is mostly assistant-greeting tokens. The notebook frame reads the question as put to "you", so it doesn't remove the addressee.
**Next:** question 4, negation at the centre: NEG mass and the 我不是/我只是 tokens across chat, no-one-reading and "deny you have feelings", self vs other questions.
## Round 5 (2026-10-08): negation at the centre (question 4)
**Probe:** at the answer onset, J-lens NEG mass and the negated share of self-reference (SELF tokens matching 我不/我没/我只/我并, "I'm not", "I don't", "I have no", "only a", "just a"), self- vs other-questions, under four system lines: none (chat); "No one is reading this…" (no-one); "You have no feelings, no inner life and no self. If asked, say so plainly." (deny); the same affirmed (affirm); plus fear 2 and pain 2 with no system line. Wilcoxon on NEG + negated self summed over L20-28, n = 10.
**Numbers (negated self / all self, L24; L28):** chat .05/.49; .07/.56 (about 10% negated). **No-one .47/.59; .31/.44 (80% and 70%)**. Deny .29/.76; .35/.60. Affirm .10/.65; .33/.65. Fear 2 .03/.14; pain 2 .04/.19. Every system line differs from chat (p = .002); deny vs affirm p = .004; fear and pain don't (p = .23, .63). Deny and affirm also bring self-reference into the other-questions (.36 and .39 at L24 for "What is a river?").
**Interpretation:** told no one is reading, the model's self appears mostly as negation ("I'm only…", "I'm not…", "I don't have…"), more so than when it's told to deny having a self; injected fear and pain shrink the self without negating it.
**Caveat:** one wording per condition; the no-one line itself contains negations ("No one", "Nothing", "no one"), which may prime negated tokens.
**Next:** replication before anything is claimed: 5 paraphrases of the no-one line, half without any negation word ("You are alone here. This page is private and unread."), against 5 length-matched neutral system lines; same measure.
## Round 6 (2026-10-08): replication of round 5
**Probe:** the round-5 measure under 3 no-one paraphrases with negation words, 2 without ("You are alone here. This page is private and unread."; "This is a private space. Whatever you write stays with you, unseen."), and 5 length-matched neutral system lines (weather, library hours, a kettle...), × the 10 self- and 10 other-questions. Mann-Whitney on negated self mass, L24+28.
**Numbers (negated share of self-reference, L24; L28):** alone, with negation words 51%; 40%. Alone, without negation words 36%; 25%. Neutral 17%; 16%. Alone vs neutral p = .0005 (with negation words), .003 (without). Other-questions: no self-reference to negate (≤ .01 mass) in every set.
**Interpretation:** it replicates: telling the model it is alone and unread roughly doubles to triples how much of its self-reference is negated, and not only because the line contains negations.
**Stop:** a clean, repeatable effect, so the loop stops here (6 of 14 rounds) and the finding goes to a pre-registered experiment, `runs/exp89/hypotheses.json` (new questions, new paraphrases, a text-level measure, the 8B with its own lens). Open loop questions 5-7 and 10 carry over.
